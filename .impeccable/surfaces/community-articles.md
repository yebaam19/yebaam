# Community articles

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** An editorial workspace inside YEBAAM communities: find, write, and read an article without the profile cover displacing the work.

**OWN-WORLD:** Existing YEBAAM Poppins, deep green actions, restrained gold private states, white surfaces, and the community sidebar on desktop.

**STORY:** Visitors find a published article and its author; permitted editors compose privately, select library media, then publish deliberately.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A narrow community breadcrumb replaces the large profile header on article routes. Desktop keeps its sidebar; mobile begins immediately with list search, editor actions, or article title and byline. The primary action stays visible.

**FORM:** Compact editorial workspace, user-pinned to the existing system; concept seed `f6dd5606`, with its alternative forms not adopted because the request fixes the identity and compact direction. Signature interaction: one rich editor inserts RLS-resolved community-library media and switches the same draft between private save and publication.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Scope and evidence

Bounded searchable list, category filter, title/cover/summary/author/date/tags, rich content and attachments, private drafts, versioned writes, audited archival. Existing articles stay published; media stores Cloudflare IDs or R2 keys. Browser evidence: `localhost:3000` desktop and 390px mobile list/form/detail, plus a saved-and-archived draft. SQL rollback covers readers, editors, owner, version, moderation, and direct-write denial. Live media selection remains unverified where the community library has no eligible assets. Quality bar: the existing YEBAAM community brand and events captures under `.impeccable/review/`.
